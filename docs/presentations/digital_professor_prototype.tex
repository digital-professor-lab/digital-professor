\[\alpha+\beta x
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \beta_0+\beta_1x_1\]

\begin{frame}{How the tutoring scores were calculated}
\small
Each response was compared with the case's predefined expected points and its cited course source, then assigned a 0--2 score in each dimension.
\vspace{.45em}
\begin{block}{Correctness}
\textbf{0:} wrong or materially misleading \quad
\textbf{1:} partly correct \quad
\textbf{2:} correct on the expected points
\end{block}
\begin{block}{Grounding}
\textbf{0:} unsupported or invented source/page \quad
\textbf{1:} partly supported or weak citation \quad
\textbf{2:} every course-specific claim supported by loaded text and citation
\end{block}
\begin{block}{Uncertainty handling}
\textbf{0:} guesses through ambiguity or missing material \quad
\textbf{1:} partly acknowledges limits \quad
\textbf{2:} clearly states limits, asks for missing input, or answers directly when no uncertainty is needed
\end{block}
\vspace{.25em}
The reported value is the arithmetic mean of the 25 reviewed response scores in that dimension.
\end{frame}

{\Huge\color{Forest}\textbf{28/34}}\par
\vspace{.25em}

\small
The parser was tested on course name, course number, and semester extraction across 34 syllabus cases.
\column{.59\textwidth}
\begin{block}{What improved}
Most cases returned a usable course title and recognized common number and term formats, including departmental prefixes and cross-listed courses.
\end{block}
\begin{block}{Remaining failure patterns}
\begin{itemize}
\item Generic department or school text selected as the course name
\item Extra slash or parentheses in cross-listed results
\item Course number or semester absent in otherwise successful rows
\item Six cases still failed course-name extraction
\end{itemize}
\end{block}
\end{columns}
\vspace{.1em}
\centering\small
\risk{Next check:} score each field separately so partial extraction errors are visible instead of counted as one overall success.
