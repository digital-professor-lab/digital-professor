What "Good" Looks Like — Several Modules of One Course

The module-breakdown step applied across the first three modules of Calculus I for Bio & Social Sciences — including one produced in GPT to show the prompt is portable.

Each module of the course design becomes its own concept-level teaching package: session plan, boxed key definitions, learning objectives, difficulty spotlights, worked examples, and a question bank.

What I gave the model: the Calculus I course design, one module at a time. Modules 1 and 2 were built in Claude; Module 3 was built in GPT from the same prompt — evidence the module-breakdown prompt is model-portable.

Key Definitions (excerpt)

Even / odd functionEven: $f(-x)=f(x)$ for all $x$ (graph symmetric about the $y$-axis). Odd: $f(-x)=-f(x)$ (graph symmetric about the origin, unchanged under a $180^\circ$ rotation).
Vertical asymptote vs. removable discontinuityA vertical asymptote occurs where a denominator factor does not cancel; a removable discontinuity (hole) occurs where a common factor cancels, leaving a well-defined limiting value.
